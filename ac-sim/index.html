Building & system inputs

Simplified hourly model: clear-sky solar geometry for the chosen date, wall and roof sol-air temperatures with a 2–3 hour thermal lag, window solar gain, ventilation and infiltration, lighting, equipment and people, and rooftop-unit efficiency that falls as outdoor temperature rises. Indoor temperature is held at setpoint, so thermal mass storage is ignored. Treat results as an estimate for comparing seasons and orientations, not a utility bill forecast.
